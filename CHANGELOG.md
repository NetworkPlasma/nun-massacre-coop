# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first public release of the multiplayer mod for Nun Massacre Definitive Edition (Windows, build 2022.06.08).

- **Up to four players**, one host and three friends: over the internet with an invite code (the host's router opens the
  port by UPnP where it can), or over a LAN or VPN. Friends can join a game that is already under way.
- **The whole game together:** the opening, every puzzle, every ending and the alternate scenarios (Carnival, Backrooms,
  PDM). Puzzles are shared, and a puzzle part mid-animation belongs to whoever is doing it. The school entrance, the
  scares and every ending wait for the whole group, then happen to everyone at once.
- **A Nun who hunts every player and hears through the building.** Raytraced acoustics (Powered by Vercidium Audio)
  carry voices, footsteps, whistles, gunshots and dropped or thrown things to her through walls, doors and floors, and
  she hears more the harder the difficulty. She stops to listen, turns to a sound, and comes to where it was made to
  search.
- **Proximity voice chat** on the same acoustics, with the space you are in answering your own voice.
- **Knocked down, not killed:** crawl, call for help and be revived (faster with a first aid kit) before a five-minute
  bleed-out. Out of lives, spectate until the next level.
- **Hide two to a bed, shout to pull the Nun off a friend, point and whistle.**
- **A tool for every player** (the lighter, the map, the bandages); the radar and the revolver stay one each for the team.
- **Teammates as Mike, Maddy or Mark** (by Vinrax), with motion-captured movement drawn in the game's own PS1 style,
  holding what they carry in the palm of their hand.
- **A clear MULTIPLAYER screen:** your name, then HOST A GAME or JOIN A GAME (with games on your own network listed); in the
  lobby, the players and how friends join, and your character, voice and microphone (shown by its full name).
- **A setup program** that finds the game, installs the BepInEx loader and the mod, updates and uninstalls, and never
  changes the game's own files.
- Network protocol 19: everyone needs 1.0.0.

The test builds before this release (0.3 to 2.0.9) are described in `docs/DEVELOPMENT-HISTORY.md` in the source package.
